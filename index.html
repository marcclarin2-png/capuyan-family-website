Our Memories

Every chapter of our lives has a story. Here are some of the moments we want to remember forever.

Our Beginning

How It All Started

It all started at work.

Joyce and I first met in the workplace. At the time, I was a Team Leader, supporting a team that was still in the training stage, while Joyce was one of the agents on that team.

At first, it was simply a professional relationship. My role was to support the team, help them through training, and make sure they had what they needed to succeed.

Little did we know that this ordinary workplace would eventually become the place where our story began.

A Special Day

Our First Birthday Together

One of the first special moments we shared together was my birthday.

It was the first time Joyce and I celebrated my birthday together. It may have seemed like a simple celebration, but looking back, it became one of those memories that I will always treasure.

It wasn't just about celebrating another year of my life. It was about having Joyce there with me and creating a memory together for the first time.

Looking back at this photo now, it reminds me of how our story was slowly becoming something more than just two people who met at work.

Our First Valentine's Day

A Simple Valentine's Celebration

Our first Valentine's celebration together was simple. We spent the day at the mall, just enjoying each other's company.

There was nothing extravagant about it, and we didn't need anything fancy. What made the day special was simply being together and having our first Valentine's memory as a couple.

Looking back at this photo, it's one of those simple moments that reminds us that happiness doesn't always come from big celebrations. Sometimes, it's just about being with the person you love.

Our First Beach Adventure

Our First Beach Outing

Our first beach outing together wasn't originally planned as a trip just for the two of us. It was actually a cluster outing with Joyce's coworkers and their bosses.

Since Joyce was part of the group, I got to be there with her and experience our first beach outing together.

We spent the day enjoying the beach, being around her coworkers, and making memories together outside of the workplace.

It was a simple outing, but for us, it became another special chapter in our story. Looking back, it's one of those moments that reminds us how our relationship slowly grew through simple experiences and shared memories.

A New Beginning

Welcome to the World, Heymie Joy

One of the most unforgettable moments of our journey was the day Joyce gave birth to our daughter, Heymie Joy.

After all the memories we had created together, this was the moment when our little family began.

Seeing our daughter for the first time was a feeling that is difficult to put into words. It was a new chapter filled with love, happiness, excitement, and a whole new kind of responsibility.

From that day on, our story was no longer just about Joyce and me. It became about the three of us.

Welcome to our story, Heymie Joy. You are one of the greatest chapters of our lives. ❤️

December 16, 2023

The Day We Said "I Do"

After all the memories, experiences, and chapters we shared together, the day finally came when Joyce and I said, "I do."

December 16, 2023, became one of the most important days of our lives. It was the day we made a promise to continue our journey together as husband and wife.

Looking back at everything that brought us to this moment— from meeting at work, to celebrating our first birthday and Valentine's Day together, to our first beach outing, and eventually becoming parents to Heymie Joy—our wedding day felt like the beginning of another beautiful chapter.

We had already built a life together. On this day, we simply made a promise to keep building it together, through every season of life.

December 16, 2023 — our forever officially began. ❤️

Our First Adventure

Our First Plane Ride Together

Our first trip together was also the first time Joyce and I ever rode a plane together.

It all started when her auntie invited us to have a vacation and celebrate with their family during their family reunion.

For both of us, riding a plane together for the first time was an exciting and unforgettable experience. It wasn't just about going somewhere new—it was about experiencing that new adventure side by side.

We are grateful to her auntie for giving us the opportunity to be part of their family reunion and for making our first trip and first plane ride together possible.

That vacation gave us more than just a trip. It gave us another beautiful memory to add to our story.

Our first flight, our first vacation, and one more adventure together. ✈️❤️

Our Family of Three

Our First Family Photoshoot

As our family grew, we wanted to capture the three of us together—Joyce, Heymie, and me.

This was our first photoshoot as a family, and having Heymie with us made it even more special. It was a chance to capture not just a picture, but a moment in our lives that we could look back on years from now.

We had already created so many memories as a couple, but this photo represented something even more meaningful: the family we had built together.

From just the two of us to a family of three. ❤️

Santiago Bay, Camotes

Our First Family Getaway

One of the memories we will always treasure is our first getaway as a family of three.

We went to Santiago Bay Beach in Camotes, and it was our first time having a little vacation with just the three of us—Joyce, Heymie, and me.

It was a simple getaway, but being able to step away from our usual routine and spend time together as a family made it special.

We enjoyed the beach, the time together, and most importantly, having a memory that belonged just to our little family.

Looking back, this trip reminds us how much our story had changed. What started with two people meeting at work had grown into a family of three, making our own memories and adventures together.

Our first family getaway. Santiago Bay, Camotes. A simple trip, but a memory we'll always carry with us. ❤️🌴

A New Beginning

A New Home, A New Beginning

After all the memories we had created together, another big change came into our lives. We decided to move to my mom's place when she decided to move out of the country.

It was more than simply moving into a different house. It was the beginning of another chapter for our little family.

We were starting to build our everyday life together in a new place, creating a home where Joyce, Heymie, and I could make even more memories.

It was another step forward in our journey as a family— another change, another challenge, and another opportunity to grow together.

Sometimes, a new chapter begins with simply finding a place to call home. ❤️🏠

Our First Achievement

Our First Car

One of the achievements we were really proud of was finally having our own car.

Having a car was something Joyce and I had always wanted. We dreamed of having one so we could travel more easily and take our little family wherever we wanted to go.

Finally getting our own car felt like a big accomplishment for us. It wasn't just about owning a vehicle. It was a symbol of the things we were working hard to achieve together.

And most importantly, it meant we could travel more comfortably with our little Mimi and create even more memories as a family.

Our first car, our first big achievement, and another dream we made real together. 🚗❤️

Boracay Family Reunion

A Heart in the Sand

When my mom came back from Canada, we had the chance to spend some quality time together as a family. We went to Boracay with my mom, my sister and her family, making it a special family reunion.

While we were enjoying the beach, Joyce's cousin created a big heart-shaped opening in the sand.

Somehow, the three of us—Joyce, Heymie, and I—ended up looking down through the heart. We took a photo from inside the sand, and it became one of those unexpected moments that turned into a beautiful family memory.

Looking at this photo now, I love how simple it is. Just the three of us, together at the beach, looking through a heart made in the sand.

A simple moment, captured in a heart, and a memory we'll always treasure. ❤️🏝️
